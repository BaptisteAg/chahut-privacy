How CHAHUT works

CHAHUT is an offline party game application. It requires no account and includes no advertising, usage analytics service or application server. The application does not transmit your game data to the publisher or a remote service.

Data used on your device

The application stores player names or nicknames and local identifiers, team names and membership, game settings, PMU game bets, your chosen language and some help display flags. This information is used to prepare and resume your game configurations.

It also stores identifiers of previously used questions and words with timestamps to reduce repetition. This history does not record your answers or a transcript of your games.

CHAHUT reads the language and region configured in your system to select the initial language and adapt the available content. This is not geolocation, and the application does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone or photos.

Storage and security

Data is stored in the private space Android assigns to CHAHUT. CHAHUT does not make this data available to other applications. Protection of this space also depends on the security of your device. CHAHUT does not add separate application-level encryption to this data.

Retention

Players, teams and settings remain stored until you change them, clear CHAHUT’s application data or uninstall it. The repetition history uses a logical thirty-day period: older entries are disregarded or cleaned up when the relevant game is used. This does not guarantee automatic physical deletion after thirty days.

Android backups and device transfers

Starting with Android version code 252, CHAHUT asks Android to exclude its data from cloud backups and device-to-device transfers through the application’s backup settings and rules. The application provides no data synchronization.

These exclusions do not remove backups that may have been created with an earlier version. Those backups are managed by your system and backup service. Transfer tools may also behave differently depending on the device manufacturer; CHAHUT cannot guarantee the behavior of all external tools.

Delete your local data

To delete all CHAHUT local data, open Android Settings, select Apps, CHAHUT, Storage and Clear storage or Clear data (wording may vary by device). This resets players, teams, settings, language and history. You can also uninstall CHAHUT. Local deletion does not necessarily remove historical backups managed by Android.

Read this policy on the web

The policy displayed in CHAHUT is bundled with the application and can be read without an internet connection. The web button opens your external browser. The public page is hosted on GitHub Pages without advertising, analytics scripts or external resources added by the publisher.

When you visit the public page, GitHub may process technical connection information, including your IP address, under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. This visit is separate from using CHAHUT offline and does not send your players, settings or history to GitHub.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact Baptiste Ageron at baptisteageron@gmail.com. If you send an email, the publisher receives the information you choose to include and uses it to handle your request. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Correspondence is retained while handling your request and any necessary follow-up.
